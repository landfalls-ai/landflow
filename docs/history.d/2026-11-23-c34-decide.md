section: 2026-11-23: Decide node
| (this change) | **Decide node (C34).** A new AI node, `core.decide`, asks a decision model Choice, Yes/No and Score questions about each item and routes it by the answer: Ollama's `nimble:9b` (no key), TypeSafe's Jev or Strands Decider. The item passes through with `decision` (probabilities per question); routing by a Choice question gives one output per option plus Unsure (below a minimum confidence), by a Yes/No question Yes and No, and the outputs follow the options as they are edited (a new port rule in the protocol, `expand: 'decide'`, used by the canvas, the Steps list, the logs and the editor). The System One client in `nodes/systemone.ts` grew from yes/no to the three types and an Ollama connection (no key, a 120 s timeout, "run `ollama pull`" and "upgrade Ollama" errors); the guardrail judge is unchanged. Calls are metered with the new purpose *Decide*. Spotlight knows the node. New fake `@landflow/test-fakes/systemone`; a live test behind `LANDFLOW_INTEGRATION=ollama`. Closes "a Decide node". | [features/ai.md](features/ai.md#decide) |
